    How your score is calculated

    WPM = correctly matched characters ÷ 5 ÷ elapsed minutes. Raw WPM counts inserted character attempts. Speed is measured after at least one second; there is no deadline.

    Accuracy is the share of correctly matched characters among reached characters and inserted extras. Corrections improve current accuracy; raw speed still counts retyped character attempts. Space (or Finish here) commits the current word: omitted letters turn red, and the next word stays aligned. Progress shows how far you reached, not how much was correct.

    Only fully completed, non-interrupted passages with at least 90% accuracy are compared for personal bests. Comparisons use the same difficulty, text style, punctuation and number settings.
